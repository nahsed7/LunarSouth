**Discrepancy analysis.** (a) Point vs pixel: AVGVISIB is a 60 m pixel average; at ridge-crest sites the 1 km p10-p90 spread (DATASET_STATISTICS.md) spans tens of percent, so a few-pixel offset explains most of the residual. (b) Observer height: model uses a 2 m mast; AVGVISIB is surface level - raising the observer increases visibility, biasing the model high (+0.029 mean for Sun). (c) Near-field relief: the horizon now merges a high-resolution 0-1 km near field (NAC 3.5 m for CR06/CR07/CR001; legacy 5/20 m LOLA grids for LM1, Site06/20/23, MMO02) with the 80/240 m far field; this cut the Site23 residual from +0.14 to +0.035 and raised overall r from 0.953 to the value above. Sites without a fine grid (MMO01, MMA03, SP09, NR201, PNS03, DGR101, HW06, MMP01) keep 80 m near field and the largest remaining residuals (MMA03, DGR101, HW06). (d) WAC counts images, not time, and does not correct non-linear temporal sampling (producer's note) and includes scattered light; it reads systematically lower than time-based models. Earth agreement is better than Sun because the Earth disc is ~4× larger and its motion is libration-driven.

## 5. Feasibility index weights (planning heuristic, not a requirement)

25% sunlit fraction · 15% longest darkness · 20% Earth visibility · 10% longest DTE blackout · 15% operational time supported (M5) · 15% terrain index (M3). Terrain index = 0.5·(% ≤10°) + 0.3·(p90-slope penalty) + 0.2·(relief penalty) at the best available resolution.
